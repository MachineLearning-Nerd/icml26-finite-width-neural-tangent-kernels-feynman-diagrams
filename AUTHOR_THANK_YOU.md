# Author thank-you note

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit of:

**Finite-Width Neural Tangent Kernels from Feynman Diagrams**
Max Guillen, Philipp Misof, and Jan E. Gerken
[arXiv:2508.11522v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.11522v4) · [OpenReview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SOlPHMdSY3)

Thank you to Max Guillen, Philipp Misof, and Jan E. Gerken for developing and
sharing a technically rich framework for making finite-width neural tangent
kernel corrections inspectable. The paper's explicit diagrammatic rules,
equations, source artifacts, and finite experiments made a claim-by-claim
independent audit possible.

This dossier is intended as a respectful companion record. It separates
executable evidence from interpretation, states the finite contracts and
estimator substitutions, preserves negative controls, and records limitations
without implying author review, endorsement, or responsibility for this
repository.
